# Music

I've been writing songs for most of my life. Some have found their way onto recordings; others are still waiting for their moment. This section collects that work — links to where you can hear it, and the lyrics themselves.

## Projects

**[New Sweat](https://www.newsweat.com)** is my band. We play rock and roll out of the Raleigh/Durham area.

My **solo work** spans a wider range — folk, rock, Americana — and is available on [Bandcamp](https://abekline.bandcamp.com/) and [Reverbnation](https://www.reverbnation.com/abekline).

Head to [Releases & Links](recordings.md) for the full list of streaming and social links.

## Lyrics

The [Lyrics section](all_i_can_say.md) contains all original lyrics, with copyright dates ranging from 2005 to the present. Some have associated recordings; some don't yet.
